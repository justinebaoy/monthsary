05 A letter

One more thing,
folded up.

Touch the envelope and the letter inside unfolds. It stays folded until then.

A letter, kept here

To My Favorite Person

My love, bebi, monik

If there's one thing I wish you could do, it's to see yourself through my eyes, even just for a moment. Maybe then you'd understand how much you truly mean to me and how deeply I love you.

I know I'm not always vocal about my feelings. Sometimes, I struggle to find the right words, and there are things inside me that I don't know how to explain. But please don't mistake my silence for a lack of love. Deep inside, it's you. It's always been you.

Even on days when things aren't easy, when we misunderstand each other or when words fail us, I still choose you. I want you to know that my love isn't only meant for the happy and easy days. I want to be there through the difficult ones, too.

I want to be your safe place, the person you can come home to when the world feels too heavy. I want you to feel that you don't always have to be strong around me. You can be vulnerable, you can have bad days, you can tell me what's bothering you, and you never have to pretend that everything is okay.

I may not always know how to express everything I feel, but I hope that through my actions, my efforts, and the way I continue to choose you, you'll eventually understand all the things my words cannot say.

If only you could see what I see when I look at you, and if only you could feel what I feel whenever I think about you. I hope you never have to question how special you are to me.

I love you, Monik. More than I sometimes know how to say.

Happy monthsary, my favorite person. Here's to us, and to all the little moments we have yet to share.

Always yours.
