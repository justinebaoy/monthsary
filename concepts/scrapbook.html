01 Pressed tulips

Three tulips,
pressed flat.

Each pressed tulip keeps a note folded behind it. Choose one and the pocket opens.

a small pocket

A note from tulip one

I hope you never doubt how loved you are. Even on ordinary days, even when nothing special happens, you will always be someone worth appreciating. I'm lucky to have you, Monik.

A note from tulip two

You don't have to be okay all the time. When things get heavy, I hope you know you can rest with me. I want to understand you, listen to you, and love you through every version of yourself.

A note from tulip three

If I could choose all over again, I'd still find my way to you. Not just for the happy moments, but for the difficult ones too. I want to keep growing with you, one day at a time.

For you, Monik,

I hope you never forget how special you are to me. Even in the quiet moments when I don't know what to say, there's so much love in my heart for you. I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even for a second, because then you'd understand why I keep choosing you.

05 A letter

Tucked in
the back.

The envelope is sealed with a tulip stamp. Touch it and the letter slides out, just for you.

A letter, kept here

My love,

I don't think I'll ever find enough words to explain everything I feel for you. Sometimes I wish I could let you look inside my heart, just so you could see how much love I have for you.

I know I have moments when I don't express myself properly. I may not always say the right things or show my feelings in the way you expect, but please know that it never means I love you any less. I am still learning how to love you better, how to understand you more, and how to make you feel the love I sometimes struggle to put into words.

I want to be someone you can find peace in. Someone you can run to when everything feels overwhelming. You don't have to hide your sadness or pretend to be okay with me. I want to know every version of you, even the ones you find difficult to show.

Thank you for being part of my life, for the memories we've made, and for all the little moments that have become special simply because they were with you.

If there's one thing I hope you carry with you, it's that you are loved, appreciated, and chosen. Even when I fall short in expressing it, my heart knows where it belongs.

Happy monthsary, my Monik.

Always yours. ❤️
