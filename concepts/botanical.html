03 A message for you

A little room
for words.

A few words for the days you need them most.

Monik, if you ever forget how much you mean to me, come back to this. You are the person I want beside me in the ordinary and the difficult, on the loud days and the quiet ones.

I know I don't always say it well. But I hope you feel it in the small things the way I listen, the way I stay, the way I keep choosing you. You are my peace, and I want to be yours.

I love you, always.

04 A letter

Folded, sealed,
for later.

Some things are easier to write than say. Open it whenever you need to hear them again.

A letter, kept here

My Monik,

I wish you could see yourself the way I do not only when you feel beautiful, but on the quiet days when you can't see it at all.

I don't always say what I feel. Sometimes I get quiet, or the words come out wrong. But my love for you has never been uncertain.

On the hard days, I'm staying. I want to be your safe place — where you can put everything down and rest.

Thank you for the little moments that became my favorite memories. I notice them. I keep them.

Happy monthsary, my love. Here's to us, and to every version still to come.

Always yours.

05 The end, for now

That is all.
For now.

Come back whenever you need a reminder. I'll be here, quietly choosing you.
